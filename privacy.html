Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Campus Marketplace (“we”, “us”) is an online marketplace where university and college students in Kenya buy and sell items and services. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider), and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile details you choose to add: phone or WhatsApp number, university, course, year of study and a short bio.
  • Listings: titles, descriptions, prices, photos, location, hostel and county that you post.
  • Messages: chat messages and photos you send to other users through the site.
  • Reports: reports you submit about listings, including the reason and any details.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and basic logs created by our hosting and database providers when you use the site.

2. Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use this information only to create your account, sign you in and show your name and picture to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers listed below. Our use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your listings and let buyers and sellers contact each other.
  • To deliver messages and notifications between users.
  • To review reports, prevent fraud and spam, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To keep the service running, fix problems and improve it.

4. What other users can see

Your listings, your name, your university, your verified-seller badge and, if you add one, your phone or WhatsApp number may be visible to other people who view your listings, so that buyers can contact you. Do not add a phone number if you do not want it shown. Chat messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation and to our moderators if a report is made.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, file storage and real-time messaging.
  • Google for sign-in, if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the website.
  • Unsplash supplies some of the photographs on the site, so your browser may contact Unsplash when those pictures load.

We may also disclose information if required by Kenyan law or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your light or dark theme. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account and content until you ask us to delete them. Deleted listings and messages are removed from our database, though copies may remain in backups for a limited time.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account and content. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time. You also have rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, including the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database access rules so that users can only reach data they are allowed to see. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. International processing

Our providers may store and process data on servers outside Kenya. We choose providers that protect personal information.

11. Children

Campus Marketplace is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you on the site.
